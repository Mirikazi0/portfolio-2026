My Approach

Research-first design.

01 / Discover

User Research

Interviews, surveys, and competitive audits to understand real user pain points before a single wireframe is drawn.

02 / Define

Journey Mapping

Synthesising research into clear user personas, journey maps, and HMW statements that guide every design decision.

03 / Design

Wireframe → Prototype

From rough sketches to interactive Figma prototypes — iterating fast and validating early before handoff.

04 / Validate

Usability Testing

Testing with real users to catch friction, measure task completion, and refine before the final handoff.
