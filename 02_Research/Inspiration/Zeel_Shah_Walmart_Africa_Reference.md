## 3. Key Design Patterns to Emulate

1. **Serif + Sans + Script Pairing:**
   - Combining a bold, classic serif headline with clean sans-serif UI body copy and informal handwritten blue script notes creates personality without sacrificing professionalism.
2. **Outcome-Oriented Headline:**
   - `"From scrolling to shopping"` immediately defines the behavioral shift (passive browsing → active purchasing).
3. **Annotated Doodles & Highlights:**
   - Hand-drawn underlines and thought bubbles (`In a love hate relationship with ai`) signal authentic craft and human design thinking.
4. **Contextual Device Presentation:**
   - Realistic device mockup anchored to a technical grid background rather than floating in generic whitespace.
