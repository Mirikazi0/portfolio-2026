The Company

Symph is a software development studio based in Cebu City, building products and platforms for clients across industries. During my 6-month internship, I was embedded within the design team — but my responsibilities extended far beyond product screens.

Aside from being a UI/UX Designer working across multiple Symph products, I was also part of the marketing creative team and served as the photographer for Symph's community events.

What I Learned

Symph gave me a front-row seat to how a real engineering-driven studio operates — and pushed me to grow across design, marketing, and documentation simultaneously.

🎨 Designing across product contexts builds adaptability. Each product had a different audience, tone, and constraint set — switching between them sharpened my ability to design with context, not just templates.

📣 Marketing creative is a design discipline. Designing pubmats and social content for Symph's brand taught me that persuasion, timing, and platform context are just as important as visual polish.

📷 Photography is documentation and storytelling. Capturing Tech Listens events wasn't just about taking photos — it was about preserving the energy, the speakers, and the community Symph was building.

🤝 Studio culture accelerates growth. Being embedded in a team of engineers, designers, and product people — all moving fast — taught me how to communicate design decisions, give and receive feedback, and ship work with confidence.
