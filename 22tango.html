The pivot

From managing artists to growing them.

22 Tango began as an artist management and promotions label in Cebu. Founder and producer Cattski Espina recognized that traditional music promotion prioritized fast visibility and commercial metrics over artistic craft.

22 Tango shifted focus to education, establishing a structured, process-first creative school for beginners and emerging artists across songwriting, vocal mastery, audio production, DJing, and hosting.

The existing digital presence reflected a music label rather than an educational institution. The brand system, website, and visual language needed to be built to support all five programs.

What needed to be built from zero

Brand identity system, responsive website, CMS blog pipeline, program collaterals, promotional video reels, mini-documentaries, motion graphics, and event photography.

The design tension

Bold enough to attract. Safe enough to stay.

22 Tango operates between rigid classical conservatories prioritizing exam scores and commercial music platforms focusing on quick metrics.

The student base includes beginners dealing with performance anxiety. The visual direction needed to convey credibility and structure without feeling intimidating.

The primary design challenge:

TOO DARK / EDGY
"This looks like a record label."

Dark aesthetics signal commercial music culture, alienating beginner students.

TOO SOFT / GENERIC
"This looks like every other music school."

Generic pastel branding loses the distinct visual identity of 22 Tango.

The resulting visual direction was Editorial Neobrutalism: high-contrast borders, structured typography, and clear card layouts positioned around a studio sanctuary concept.

Design decisions

Three calls that shaped everything.

1.
Color-coded program identity.

Assigning specific color palettes to each program gave students and instructors a dedicated visual anchor for their track.

2.
Process-driven content hierarchy.

The site architecture prioritizes the Learning Pledge and mentorship model over standard instructor bios, highlighting the process-first philosophy.

3.
CMS pipeline for non-technical instructors.

Integrating Decap/Netlify CMS allows instructors to publish articles and program updates directly without code changes.

The hard call

Resisting the aesthetic they were used to.

The brief pulled dark.
The students needed light.

Early concepts leaned toward dark, high-contrast layouts typical of music labels and event posters.

Visual audits of competing programs indicated that dark schemes signaled exclusivity to beginners. Shifting to a bright, structured layout balanced professional credibility with an approachable tone.

The resulting design established a clear, accessible entry point for new students.

The principle behind the call
