Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage are small text fragments stored on your device when visiting websites to help remember settings, sessions, or user preferences.

2. Our Minimal Storage Usage

This portfolio website believes in a privacy-first web experience:

  • No Advertising / Tracking Cookies: We do NOT use marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • Essential UI Preference Storage: We use lightweight browser `localStorage` (e.g. key `cookie_consent_accepted`) exclusively to remember whether you have acknowledged the privacy notification banner.

3. Managing Storage Preferences

You can clear local storage or block cookies at any time via your web browser settings. Clearing local storage will simply cause the privacy preference banner to reappear on your next visit.
