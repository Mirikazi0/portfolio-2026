## 📌 Job Description Summary

* **Company Culture:** B-Corp certified marketing agency based in Vancouver, Canada, creating engaging digital experiences across industries.
* **Core Focus:** Polished, conversion-focused creative for clients—website visuals, style guides, and marketing assets with light UI/UX thinking.
* **Key Responsibilities:**
  1. Design website visuals, landing pages, and marketing creative (ads, emails, social).
  2. Develop Figma style guides (typography, color systems, layouts, visual direction).
  3. Design pages with conversion (CRO) and usability in mind.
  4. Produce wireframes and layout concepts.
  5. Collaborate with developers to translate visuals into responsive designs.
  6. Support performance marketing teams with design needs.
* **Required Skills:** Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, typography, layout, visual hierarchy, responsive web constraints.
* **Nice-to-haves:** Agency experience, Webflow, Motion design, CRO / performance creative.
