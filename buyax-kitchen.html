1. Background & Challenge

BUYAX Kitchen is a popular local MSME food establishment known for its cozy, rustic dining vibe downstairs and a lively weekend rooftop lounge upstairs. Serving a diverse menu ranging from artisanal pizzas and tortillas to shawarmas and hearty rice meals, Buyax Kitchen attracts dating couples, youth, and family gatherings.

The Core Challenge: Despite high customer satisfaction with food quality, Buyax Kitchen struggled with visual inconsistencies:
• The original logo was informally designed on Canva and hadn't evolved with the business.
• Packaging lacked brand identity — specialty items like shawarmas and tortillas were served in plain aluminum foil.
• Text-only menus caused ordering confusion and failed to showcase signature dishes visually.
• Inconsistent promotional graphics across Instagram and Facebook created a fragmented brand image.

2. Brand Identity & Logo Redesign

The rebranding aimed to craft an inviting, memorable identity that bridges the warm vintage aesthetic of the main dining room with the modern energy of the rooftop space.

The new logo system transitions away from generic Canva badge graphics into a refined, handcrafted wordmark and emblem badge. The design retains a warm culinary charm while providing a versatile visual anchor across digital avatars, packaging stickers, uniform embroidery, and storefront signage.

3. Packaging Suite & Menu Redesign

To eliminate informal packaging practices, a dedicated food packaging system was developed:

Custom Wrappers & Liners

Branded food-grade greaseproof wrapping paper replacing plain foil for shawarmas and tortillas.

Pizza & Takeout Boxes

Custom-printed eco-friendly craft boxes featuring bold brand typography and badge iconography.

Sticker & Label System

Versatile adhesive seals for beverage cups, takeout bags, and container closures.

Visual Menu & Food Photography

Restructured menu layout incorporating studio food photography to streamline ordering.
