1. Studio Vision & Mission

Make It Happen Studio is a craft-led creative execution practice founded to help early-stage founders bridge the gap between idea and tangible product. Rather than delivering endless strategy decks or unbuilt clickable mockups, the studio's core promise is action: building and shipping working MVPs that founders can grow from.

Brand Values: Plainspoken. Crafted. Confident.
The visual identity needed to communicate three key feelings in order:
1. Trust & Taste: "This person knows what she's doing."
2. Approachable & Transparent: "I can actually afford and access this." (No corporate agency fluff).
3. Execution-Focused Energy: "This is going to be fun." (Warmth and action-first momentum).

2. Logotype System & Brand Patterns

Because "Make It Happen Studio" is a four-word title, a flexible multi-tier logotype system was designed:

• Full Combination Mark: Used for major touchpoints, website headers, and client proposal covers.
• Icon & Monogram Mark: Scalable icon designed for strict spaces like favicons, social avatars, and stamps.
• Ascending Brand Pattern: An echoing geometric pattern created by repeating the icon mark in an ascending sequence, symbolizing continuous growth, forward momentum, and progress.

3. Multi-Color Palette & Typography

Unlike rigid two-color corporate brands, Make It Happen Studio employs a vibrant multi-color system led by a primary teal and energetic accent tones:

Typography System: The brand pairs Momo Trust Sans as its primary clean workhorse typeface for body and titles, alongside Blocky as a bold display accent font used sparingly for high-impact headlines.

The identity system was applied across all key client touchpoints and promotional channels:

Social Media Suite

Instagram publication grids, X/Threads headers, and story templates.

Client Handoff Templates

Transparent proposal decks, invoices, and codebase handoff documentation.

Digital Touchpoints

Favicon suite, browser assets, and makeithappenstudio.io website styling.

Studio Apparel Merch

T-shirt merchandise and sticker badges for founder giveaways.
