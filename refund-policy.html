Service & Refund Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Nature of Services

Chaz Grant Borromeo provides custom design engineering, UI/UX design, design system creation, and full-stack web development services on a project contract, milestone, or retainer basis.

2. Deposit & Milestone Payments

Unless explicitly specified otherwise in a signed client statement of work (SOW) or master services agreement (MSA):

  • Project Upfront Deposits: Initial deposits/retainers required to secure project schedule and commence discovery work are non-refundable once work has officially begun.
  • Milestone Deliverables: Payment for completed and accepted project milestones (e.g. design systems, approved UI mockups, deployed web builds) is non-refundable upon client review sign-off.

3. Project Cancellation & Terminations

If a project is paused or terminated by the client prior to completion, the client will only be billed for hours logged or milestone phases completed up to the date of written cancellation notice. Any unearned portion of prepaid retainers beyond active work logged will be refunded within 14 business days.

4. Revision & Satisfaction Guarantee

Every freelance design engineering project includes dedicated revision rounds as stipulated in the project contract to ensure deliverables meet high technical and visual standards prior to final sign-off.

5. Billing Inquiries

For questions regarding contract terms, retainer billing, or milestone sign-offs, please email chazgrantborromeo@gmail.com.
